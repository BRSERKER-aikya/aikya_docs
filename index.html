Sufi Culture & The 7 Stages of Love

In Sufi culture, love is not just an emotion; it is a spiritual journey comprising 7 distinct stages of love (Maqaam) that lead the soul from earthly attraction to ultimate annihilation in the divine:

  • 1. Dil (Will / Attraction): The initial spark and inclination of the heart towards beauty.
  • 2. Ishq (Love): Deep, consuming passion where the heart is entirely wrapped up in the beloved.
  • 3. Zuhd (Adoration / Reverence): Moving past ordinary affection into profound devotion and awe.
  • 4. Ma'rifat (Understanding / Knowledge): Recognizing the deeper truth and essence behind the beloved.
  • 5. Wajd (Ecstasy): Losing control to overwhelming emotional and spiritual intensity.
  • 6. Qurb (Proximity / Nearness): Feeling an intense, transcendent closeness to the divine or the beloved.
  • 7. Fana (Annihilation / The 7th Mukaam): Total dissolution of the ego and self—burning completely away in love until only the beloved remains.
